Random Chests Used adds together the random chests used for this item plan. This includes random chests entered in Chests and random chests automatically applied from Top Up Bundles.

Choice Chests Used adds together the choice chests used for this item plan. Manual choice chests come from the Chests tab. Choice chests from Top Up Bundles are added to the Choice Chest Bank first, then can be redeemed into this item plan or another item plan.

Quick Adjust lets you change manual chest inputs directly from Results. Manually added random chests and manually added choice chests can be quick adjusted. Random chests automatically applied from Top Up Bundles cannot be quick adjusted; remove or change the associated Top Up Bundle instead.

Choice chests redeemed from the Choice Chest Bank can be quick adjusted. If you reduce a redeemed bank choice chest, it is returned to the Choice Chest Bank. To remove choice chests from the Choice Chest Bank entirely, remove the associated Top Up Bundle that created them.

Choice chests in the Choice Chest Bank are not locked to the item plan that earned them. They can be applied to any item plan.

Hide Chests Used hides the chest-used summary sections above the main results for cleaner scrolling.

Estimated Target Items From Plan shows estimated duplicate target items gained from this item plan. Random chests use the game drop rate for the selected item. Choice chests are included directly because they are guaranteed duplicate items.

Remaining Items Needed shows how many more duplicate items are needed to reach the selected target level. Because choice chests are guaranteed duplicate items, this section can also be read as the number of choice chests needed. For example, if you need 9 more Level 5 items, then 9 Level 5 choice chests would complete that requirement.

Remaining Chests Needed estimates how many random chests are needed based on the selected item’s drop rate. Each value assumes only that chest level will be used. If you add choice chests or other random chests, this section updates automatically.

Left-side items have a 2/9 drop rate. Right-side items have a 1/9 drop rate. For example, if you are working on Attackers Claw, which is a right-side item, then 1 Level 5 choice chest is roughly equal to 9 Level 5 random chests for that target item.

Random Chests can give both target and non-target items.

Target items are shown in Estimated Target Items From Plan. Non-target items are shown here.

Non-target items are split by side. If the selected item is on the left, left-side non-target items are split between the other 2 left-side items, while right-side non-target items are split between the 3 right-side items. If the selected item is on the right, the logic reverses.

Items that can be assigned to a specific Raven item are credited in New Estimated Inventory. Items that cannot be logically assigned to a specific item are shown as Random Items Left Over.

Left-side items have a drop rate of 2/9, while right-side items have a drop rate of 1/9. So for every 9 random chests that are opened, with a large enough sample size, you would have approximately 2 of each left-side item and 1 of each right-side item.
